# portraits-from-my-jungle
## How the fictional beasts’ images sounds and language was generated:
### Image generation:
Two datasets of animals labeled by species were combined and prepared for the training . The Stable Diffusion v1-5 model was imported to Google Colab and trained with the datasets. Ater the training, 16 different animal names or combinations were given as prompts, and 3 variations were generated for each prompt. The process was repeated three times for a total of 150 generations. The best generations were kept, as well as their corresponding names/prompts for the sound and text generations.
### Sound generation:
The Stable Audio 3 small-music model was used to generate all of the sounds. For each of the 16 chosen images, the image prompts were used as inspiration to describe the sounds of each imaginary animal. Three sound variations were created for each animal and the preferred generations were selected for each, a 120 second ambient jungle soundscape was also generated for continuous background noise.
### Language generation:
The text for each animal was generated by Codex based on the name/prompt of the animal, the model was asked to imagine how the sound of the animal in question would be written like.

## AI assistance used:
ChatGPT (free) assisted in coding and debugging the Google Colab notebook files. The VS Code Codex (free) extension assisted in writing the webpage files (HTML, JS, CSS) and writing the text lines for each animal.
